# Surface: Ops /admin console

<!-- impeccable:surface-brief -->

## Mode

Operate

## Job

Staff operators manage gym users, inspect SQLite health/usage, triage feedback, and (when self-hosting LiveKit) open Redis Commander — without mixing ops credentials into trainee gym accounts.

## Direction

Desktop-first Liquid Glass sidebar shell (void black + lime rarity). Dense tables and KPI tiles; no Dock & Stage. English ops chrome.

## Primary targets

- `admin/index.html`, `admin/login.html`
- `src/admin/*`
- `/api/ops/*`
